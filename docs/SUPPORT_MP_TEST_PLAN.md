## Exit decisions and fail-closed behavior

For MS01/MS03 failures: stop affected support code before mutation; do not consume/refund/reset or replay to mask divergence. For MS02: leave vanilla loot intact rather than add an officer spawn or broaden eligibility. For MS04: no damage/delete/player-spoof fallback. For MS05: stop unsafe audio/attraction adapter rather than rebroadcast from clients. Retain exact FAIL evidence and known limitations; no unavailable environment becomes PASS/N/A.

First complete release requires every applicable dedicated acceptance case and all project gates. Source/offline/SP/host tests do not satisfy G7. No G7 N/A for this shared gameplay system. Required authorization/authority failures also block any later test distribution; a Workshop testing policy does not make unsafe gameplay acceptable. Preparing a release/package or publishing remains separately authorized work.
