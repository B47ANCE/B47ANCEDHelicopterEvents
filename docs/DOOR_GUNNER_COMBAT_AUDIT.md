## Targeting and ammunition design for review

Server selects currently loaded, alive, ground-level zombies within the reviewed engagement geometry near both requesting player and simulated aircraft. Reject invalid/removed/transferred owner references before each shot. Threat priority needs server-observable evidence, deterministic tie-breaks and bounded work; never trust a client list or scan every world zombie every tick. Roofs/floors/indoor cover, forest, windows, vehicles and line of fire need an explicit verified visibility/collision path. A simple 2D radius is insufficient to shoot through buildings. Until verified, reject ambiguous targets rather than assume penetration.

Server alone resolves accuracy/misses, rounds per burst, fire interval and finite ammo. Each scheduled shot consumes its resolved ammunition regardless of hit; snapshots/burst presentation cannot reroll outcomes or reset ammo. Expiry/ammo exhaustion causes departure; cancellation ends firing immediately. Initial design has no player targeting/friendly fire. Do not create a real weapon item in player inventories just to impersonate a shooter. Damage multipliers, burst length, interval and range are meaningful sandbox candidates; technical caps/IDs remain internal. No values were implemented.

## Era and sound selection gate

The Army's [M60D equipment reference](https://odin.t2com.army.mil/WEG/Asset/9944a9ea32f351e0d75883ccbdd5d64f) identifies the 7.62mm M60D and UH-60/M144 mounting. Its [2007 aviation upgrade report](https://www.army.mil/article/1348/78th_aviation_battalion_upgrades_crew_machine_guns) describes replacement of M60D crew weapons. These support M60D as a historical candidate, **not proof of a selected aircraft's exact 1993 configuration**. No aircraft/platform/unit/loadout or matching vanilla M60 sound was finalized. A bounded generated-item scan found no named M60 definition; that does not prove all game sound banks lack a suitable cue. MS06 stays open; do not label an arbitrary rifle recording as an authentic M60D or copy a third-party weapon mod.
